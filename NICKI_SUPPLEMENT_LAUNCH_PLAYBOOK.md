## Foundation: Economics First

Before spending a dollar, get these numbers right.

### Margin Gate
> *"If your product has 70%+ gross margin you can probably make DTC work; at 40% margin it's going to be really hard."* — Matt Bertulli

- **Target:** 70%+ gross margin on supplement SKUs
- Creatine + collagen are relatively low-COGS ingredients — achievable
- Build your 3PL, packaging, and COGS spreadsheet before touching ads

### Unit Economics Model
```
Monthly subscription: $49–$79/month
Quarterly option: $129–$189 (key unlock — see below)
One-time: $59–$99

Target CPA: $40–$70 (supplements, cold traffic)
Target CAC payback: 90 days max → collect $129 quarterly upfront against $60 CPA = immediate payback
```

**The Quarterly Option Unlock** (Nik Sharma):
Adding a quarterly billing option ($43/month billed as one payment) against a $60–80 CPA means you collect cash upfront, immediately turn profitable on acquisition, and can scale Meta spend aggressively. This single pricing lever changes the entire business model.

### Cash Conversion Reality
- Budget $5K–$100K before knowing if DTC works
- Know your: average inventory turn, cash-to-sale cycle, per-customer upfront cash requirement
- Target first-order contribution margin of 0 (break even) — all profit lives in LTV

## Phase 0: Pre-Launch (8–12 Weeks Out)

### 0.1 Positioning Lock

**ICP Statement to nail before everything else:**
> Think: *"Functional gear for the stylish woman"* — not for the girl who doesn't care how she looks, but also not purely aesthetic.

For creatine + collagen this means:
- Not "gym bro supplements for women"
- Not "basic collagen powder"
- **Position as:** Beauty performance — the thing that makes you look AND perform better. Creatine for mental clarity + muscle tone; collagen for skin, hair, nails, joints.

**Differentiation angles to test (run as pre-launch ad creative):**
1. "You've been taking the wrong supplement" (challenge category)
2. "Beauty from the inside out" (aspirational)
3. "The supplement gap — what women actually need" (education)
4. "I finally feel like myself again" (transformation story)
5. Cost-per-day framing: "$1.43/day" vs. "$2/day" — makes price feel trivial

### 0.2 List Building (Start 12 Weeks Out — Non-Negotiable)

> *"If you're not pulling them into your email list, you're building your business on rented land."* — Chase Dimond

> *"Build email list 3-4 months before launch so you have 10,000–100,000 emails to activate on day one."* — Nik Sharma

**List building system — run all simultaneously:**

1. **Pre-launch landing page** — Single page, 1 CTA: "Join the waitlist. First to know, first to shop." Collect email + SMS opt-in. No discount needed — use exclusivity.

2. **Quiz funnel** — "What's your supplement gap?" or "Which beauty supplement is right for you?" — Quiz → email capture → personalized product recommendation. Delay email capture to near the end of quiz for higher completion rates.

3. **Organic social + founder content** — Nicki posts raw, authentic content about why she's launching this. Founder content builds trust faster than any ad. Start now, not at launch.

4. **Meta mid-funnel spend** — Run 20% of Meta budget to quiz completions and email capture (mid-funnel event optimization) before running purchase campaigns. Builds list while testing creative.

5. **Seeding early** — Send product (or teaser box) to 500+ micro-creators 8–10 weeks out. Volume is the game. 500 packages sent → meaningful content volume. 10 packages sent → maybe 1 post.

**List size goals:**
- 8 weeks out: 500+ emails
- 4 weeks out: 2,000+ emails
- Launch day: 5,000+ emails (goal — more is always better)
- SMS list: build in parallel, target 30–40% of email list size

### 0.3 Creator Seeding Strategy

> *"Seeding four audience types: creators (post regardless of following), influencers (audience connection), celebrities (high eyeballs), editors (media company push)."* — Nik Sharma

**Seeding tier system for Nicki's launch:**

| Tier | Who | Goal | Volume |
|------|-----|------|--------|
| **A: Micro-Creators** | 5K–50K followers, fitness/beauty/wellness | TikTok/IG posts, authentic UGC | 200–300 units |
| **B: Mid-Tier Influencers** | 50K–500K followers | Paid integration + affiliate code | 20–30 creators |
| **C: Creator Friends** | Whoever loves Nicki's product first | Gift bundle to their whole network | 10–15 bundles |
| **D: Editors/Press** | Beauty/wellness publications | Reviews, roundups, features | 20–30 editors |

**The friend-network gifting hack** (Nik Sharma):
Find one influencer who genuinely loves the product → create a high-value gift bundle → send it to ALL their influencer friends as a gift FROM that influencer. They will all post. One relationship multiplies into 15–20 posts.

**Affiliate setup (pre-launch):**
- Give every seeded creator a custom code (10% off for their audience, $5–$10 commission per sale)
- Tracks ROI, creates performance alignment, no upfront guaranteed payment needed
- TikTok Shop affiliate program: set up before launch (can take up to a month for supplement approval)

### 0.4 Reviews Before You Launch

> *"First time launching with 70 reviews per product at launch by shipping samples to customer advisory board beforehand."* — marketing_operator

- Build a 50-person advisory board from Nicki's existing community
- Send product 6–8 weeks before launch
- Collect reviews via Okendo/Yotpo/Stamped before day 1
- Launch with ≥50 reviews per SKU — social proof at launch dramatically lifts conversion

### 0.5 Pre-Launch Creative Testing

> *"Run ads with email/SMS submission as conversion objective to understand which headlines, imagery, video, copy convert best — then use those insights to inform homepage and product page copy."* — Nik Sharma

Run pre-launch landing page ads with **email capture as the conversion event** (not purchase). This:
- Tells you exactly which angles, hooks, and images convert
- Builds the list at the same time
- Informs what goes on the actual product pages and homepage
- Costs a fraction of conversion campaigns to learn

**Test these creative variables:**
- 2–3 headline angles (educational vs. transformation vs. challenge)
- Static vs. video
- UGC/founder style vs. polished
- With vs. without ingredient explanation

## Phase 1: Launch (Week 0–4)

### 1.1 Landing Page Architecture

> *"Modular landing page approach drives 9–10%+ conversion rates vs. sending traffic to a generic homepage."* — Nik Sharma

**Must-have LP elements (from Nik's 40-element LP framework):**
- Social proof above the fold (review count + star rating)
- Statistics-driven headline ("X% of women are deficient in...")
- Lifestyle + clinical press reviews
- Icon-based benefit section (4–6 benefits, visual)
- "Autoship" / subscribe-and-save language prominent
- Two-person offer ("Buy for you + a friend")
- Cost-per-day pricing ("$1.43/day")
- Prominent nutrition label / ingredient transparency
- Upper-funnel friendly CTAs (not just "Buy Now" — "Start your routine")
- Multiple review types: star ratings, UGC photos, video testimonials

**Segment your landing pages:**
- LP 1: Paid social traffic (general)
- LP 2: Creator/influencer traffic (matches creator's angle)
- LP 3: VIP/waitlist early access (no discount needed — exclusivity)
- LP 4: Returning visitor / retargeting (knows brand, needs nudge)

### 1.2 Launch Week Email Cadence

**Pre-Launch (Days -3 to -1):**
- **Email 1 (Day -3):** "It's almost here — here's what's coming" [blurry product teaser image, build anticipation]
- **Email 2 (Day -1, VIP only — top 10% by engagement):** "You get early access — shop now before we open to everyone"

**Launch Week:**
- **Email 3 (Day 0, AM):** Launch announcement — full reveal, story, why this product, strong offer
- **Email 4 (Day 0, PM):** Social proof wave — "here's what our advisory board said after 6 weeks"
- **Email 5 (Day 1):** Ingredient education — "what creatine actually does for women's brains + bodies"
- **Email 6 (Day 3):** UGC + creator content roundup — "creators are already posting about this"
- **Email 7 (Day 5):** Urgency + last-chance framing if any launch offer is running
- **Email 8 (Day 7):** Post-launch recap — results, what's next, subscription offer

> *"One email is never enough. Don't assume one email is sufficient during key moments — customers signed up to hear from you, keep showing up until they convert."* — Nik Sharma

### 1.3 Subscription Offer Structure

Lead with subscription on the product page. Structure:

```
□ Subscribe & Save — $49/month (save 20%)  ← DEFAULT SELECTED
□ Try Once — $59
□ 3-Month Supply — $129 (save 27%)  ← KEY UNLOCK
```

Present subscription as default. Show one-time as secondary. Calculate total annual savings clearly.

**At checkout:** Surface the quarterly option. Headline: "Get 3 months for the price of 2.3 — and never run out."

### 1.4 Paid Acquisition — First 30 Days

**Meta structure (from marketing_operator two-tier system):**

**Tier 1 — Testing (ABO):**
- $250/day budget per concept
- One concept, three creative variations
- Wait for $1K spend OR 5 days before cutting
- Test 3–4 concepts per week max

**Tier 2 — Scaling (CBO):**
- Move proven concepts here
- Minimum spends across ~3 ad sets
- 12 ads per concept in rotation
- Never touch winning ads — only add new ones

**Creative types to test week 1:**
1. Founder story (Nicki explains why she created this)
2. Before/after transformation story (emotional, not body)
3. "3 things I wish I knew before starting creatine" (education hook)
4. UGC style — real customer filming themselves
5. Side-by-side ingredient comparison (us vs. them)
6. The "scroll stopper" — counterintuitive hook ("Stop taking collagen alone")

**TikTok Shop — launch simultaneously:**
- Set up affiliate program before launch
- Spark Ads: boost any organic creator content that performs
- TikTok subscription feature: offer first-order discount for subscribers

### 1.5 Organic Content Plan (Launch Month)

Post every day across: TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts

**Content mix:**
- 40% education (what creatine/collagen actually do, ingredient deep dives)
- 30% founder/behind-the-scenes (authentic, raw, builds trust)
- 20% transformation stories (customer results, advisory board feedback)
- 10% product (demos, unboxings, routine integration)

**Cross-post everything:** One video → TikTok + IG Reels + YouTube Shorts. Same content, platform-native captions.

**TikTok SEO:** Optimize with keyword-rich captions. People search TikTok for "creatine for women," "collagen benefits," "beauty supplements." Treat it like a search engine.

## Phase 2: Post-Launch (Week 5+)

### 2.1 Welcome Flow (All New Customers)

> *"Well-built flows generate 37% of total email revenue without any ongoing campaign work."* — Chase Dimond

**6-email welcome flow:**

| Email | Timing | Content |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Immediate | Warm welcome + Nicki's origin story + what to expect |
| 2 | 48 hours | Benefits vs. competition + light offer (free shipping on next order) |
| 3 | Day 5 | Best practices for taking creatine + collagen + customer results |
| 4 | Day 8 | Reviews, UGC, influencer posts, press coverage |
| 5 | Day 12 | Strong offer + subscription upgrade pitch |
| 6 | Day 21 | Introduce second SKU / bundle offer |

### 2.2 Post-Purchase Sequence (Subscriber Retention)

The #1 place supplements lose subscribers is between order 1 and order 2.

**Retention sequence:**
- Day 1: Order confirmation + how to take your supplements correctly
- Day 3: Product education email — "here's what's happening in your body right now"
- Day 7: Check in + ask for a review
- Day 14: Share their progress path — "By week 4, most women report..."
- Day 20 (before day 30 renewal): Renewal reminder + option to pause, skip, or adjust
- Day 25: Surprise bonus announced for next box (reduces churn)
- Day 30: Order ships — excitement email + what to expect in month 2

**The "pause vs. cancel" lever:**
Always offer pause as an alternative to cancel in the cancellation flow. Pause converts 15–25% of would-be cancellations into delays.

### 2.3 Subscription Churn Prevention

**Early warning signs to watch:**
- Customer skips an order (at-risk signal)
- Customer opens emails but doesn't click (disengaging)
- Customer downgraded from quarterly to monthly

**Proactive intervention emails:**
- "We noticed you haven't opened your last shipment — are you on track?"
- "Feeling like it's not working? Here's what the research says about timing..."
- "We'd rather pause your subscription than lose you"

### 2.4 LTV Expansion

**Expand AOV post-purchase:**
- Day 45: Introduce complementary SKU (if creatine customer → introduce collagen, and vice versa)
- Day 60: Bundle offer — "Complete Beauty Stack" at a discount vs. individual SKUs
- Day 90: Replenishment reminder if not subscribed

**Referral program (launch at Day 60):**
- Referral programs generate customers at ~$16 CAC vs. $48 from paid
- Structure: "Give a friend $15 off, get $15 credit"
- Surface in post-purchase emails and the customer portal

**Loyalty program:**
- Points on every subscription order
- Creates a switching cost — customers don't want to lose accumulated points
- Redemptions: free products, merch, early access to new SKUs

### 2.5 Paid Retention Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | What It Tells You |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| 30-day retention | 70%+ | Initial product satisfaction |
| 60-day retention | 50%+ | Habit formation happening |
| 90-day retention | 40%+ | True believers forming |
| LTV (12-month) | 3–4x CPA | Healthy subscription economics |
| nCAC trend | Flat or declining | Efficiency of acquisition improving |

> *"If 60-day retention improves from 20% to 25%, that compounds dramatically into LTV."* — Matt Bertulli
